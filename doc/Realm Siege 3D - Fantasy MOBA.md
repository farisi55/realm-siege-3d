# Realm Siege 3D

Choose your hero, then destroy the enemy core

Instructions

Arrow keys: move. Left click: attack an enemy or move. Right click: move. Q W E R: abilities (R unlocks at level 3). Space: center camera. B: shop. Tab: scoreboard. M: minimap size. Esc: pause. Mouse wheel: zoom. Middle-mouse drag: rotate camera. Screen edge: scroll camera. Destroy towers with your creeps, then the enemy core.
